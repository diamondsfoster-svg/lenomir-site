Privacy Policy – Lenomir

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Lenomir is created by Lenomir Labs ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy and designed Lenomir to work offline without collecting personal app data from users.

This Privacy Policy explains what stays on your device, what Lenomir Labs does not collect, and the limited standard analytics that Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console may provide outside the app.

1. Who we are

Data Controller:
Lenomir Labs
Email: support@lenomirlabs.com

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us at the email above.

2. What Lenomir is (and is not)

Lenomir is a wellness and self-support app. It provides tools for:

  • Daily trackers (mood, energy, lifestyle, body rhythm)
  • Journaling, dream records, reflections, and optional voice notes
  • Regulation tools (such as grounding, bilateral focus, quick resets, pattern reset guides, self-compassion, and inner child reflection)
  • Goals and habits for small, repeatable support practices
  • Vela reflections, which are offline, pre-structured responses based on tracker patterns

Lenomir does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services and is not a replacement for professional mental health or medical care.

3. Information Collection

Lenomir Labs does not collect personal data from users through the app. You do not need an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, tracker entries, journal entries, dream records, voice notes, reflections, health information, or other personal app content.

3.1 Information you create in the app

You may choose to create content such as:

  • Tracker entries
  • Journal entries and reflections
  • Dream Records
  • Optional voice notes or audio updates
  • Regualation and module activity
  • Goals, habits, notes, affirmations, and settings

Store Analytics

Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console may provide Lenomir Labs with standard store analytics, such as app downloads, installs, crashes, device or operating-system summaries, region-level trends, and other aggregated performance information.

These analytics are provided by Apple and Google through their developer platforms. Lenomir does not add its own tracking inside the app, and Lenomir Labs does not use this information to identify individual users.

3.3 No accounts or names

Lenomir does not require a user account. We do not ask for or collect your name, username, email address, profile, contact list, precise location, or payment information inside the app.

Any changes will be updated in this Privacy Policy.

4. How your data is stored

Lenomir stores your app content locally on your phone or tablet using device storage. Your trackers, journals, dream records, optional voice notes, saved reflections, goals, habits, and settings remain on your device unless you choose to export, back up, or share them yourself.

If you delete the app or reset local data in Settings, locally stored Lenomir data is removed from that device and is not recoverable by Lenomir Labs

5. How we use your information

Lenomir processes your entries locally on your device so the app can show your trackers, history, saved reflections, trends, goals, habits, and restoration tools.

Vela reflections are offline, pre-structured responses based on tracker data stored on your device. Your tracker data is not sent to Lenomir Labs or outside services for these reflections.

Lenomir Labs does not sell your data, does not build advertising profiles, and does not use your personal wellness content for marketing or tracking.

6. Offline reflections and no outside processing

Lenomir is intended to work as an offline app. The app does not send your personal entries, trackers, journal content, dream records, voice notes, or saved reflections to Lenomir Labs or outside services.

Vela is an offline reflection feature. It uses pre-structured responses based on tracker patterns already stored on your device. It is for supportive reflection only and is not medical, legal, safety, or professional advice.

6.1 No sale of personal data

Lenomir Labs does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal app data. We also do not use your wellness data to build advertising profiles or target you with ads.

7. Legal bases for processing (for users in the EU/EEA/UK)

Because Lenomir Labs does not collect personal app data through Lenomir, we generally do not process your personal app content as a data controller. Your entries are processed locally on your device to provide the features you choose to use.

Standard store analytics from Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console may be provided to Lenomir Labs under Apple and Google developer platform terms.

8. Data retention

Your Lenomir data stays on your device until you delete it, reset local data in Settings, or remove the app, subject to your device and backup settings. Lenomir Labs does not retain copies of your trackers, journals, voice notes, reflections, goals, habits, or settings because we do not collect them.

Apple and Google may retain store analytics according to their own policies.

9. Sharing your information

Lenomir Labs does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal app data.

The app may allow you to export or share content yourself. If you choose to export, back up, or share your own data, you control that action and the destination.

Apple and Google may provide standard app-store analytics through their developer platforms.

10. International data transfers

Lenomir Labs does not transfer your personal app content internationally because we do not collect it from the app.

Apple and Google may process standard store analytics in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights over personal data. Because Lenomir Labs does not collect your personal app content, most access, correction, deletion, and portability actions happen directly on your device.

You can exercise many of these rights directly in the App (for example, by clearing data in Settings). You can also contact us at support@lenomirlabs.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

12. Children’s privacy

Lenomir is intended for adults and older teenagers who can consent to using wellness tools under the laws of their country. It is not directed to children under the age where parental consent is required.

If we later introduce a separate version of Lenomir specifically for children and caregivers, it will have its own tailored privacy terms.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. However, no app or online service can guarantee absolute security.

You can support your own privacy by:

  • Using device-level security (such as a passcode or biometric lock)
  • Not sharing your device with others without proper permissions
  • Keeping your operating system and the App up to date

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the “Last updated” date at the top
  • Where appropriate, notify you in the App (for example, via a notice or prompt)

Your continued use of the App after changes are published means you accept the updated Policy.
